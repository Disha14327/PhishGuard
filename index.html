💡 Cyber Safety Tips

Build safer online habits with these simple tips.

🔍

Check Before Clicking

Look carefully at website addresses before opening links.

🔑

Protect Passwords

Use strong, unique passwords and never share them with strangers.

🔐

Never Share OTPs

Never disclose an OTP, PIN or banking password to anyone.

🎁

Question Free Offers

Be careful with unexpected prizes, rewards and unbelievable deals.

📲

Verify the Sender

Contact organisations using their official websites or apps.

🛡️

Update Your Devices

Install security updates and use trusted security software.

⚙️ How It Works

Understand the process behind the PhishGuard checker.

📥

01. Enter Information

Paste a URL or suspicious message into the appropriate scanner.

🔎

02. Pattern Checking

The checker examines the input for selected suspicious keywords and URL patterns.

📊

03. Risk Calculation

Detected warning signs contribute to a rule-based risk score.

🚨

04. View the Result

You receive a risk category, detected warning signs and safety recommendations.
